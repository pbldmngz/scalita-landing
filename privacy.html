Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Scalita ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Scalita (the "App") or our website, scalita.com.

Our Privacy Commitment

Scalita is designed with privacy in mind. We believe you should be able to warm up your voice without being tracked, profiled, or monetized. Here's our commitment:

  • No accounts required - You don't need to create an account or sign in to use Scalita.
  • No personal data collection - We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • No tracking or analytics - We don't use any analytics services, tracking pixels, or user behavior monitoring.
  • No ads - We don't display advertisements and don't share data with ad networks.
  • Offline-first - The app works entirely offline. Your data never leaves your device.

Data Storage

Everything you create in Scalita is stored locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Your vocal range and register settings
  • Custom scales you create
  • Custom routines you create
  • Practice takes you record in pitch targeting, and the audio of any harmony you save
  • Your practice history: the days you practised, your pitch scores, and your Humming Bird bests
  • App preferences (language, notation style, tempo settings)

This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties, because we do not have servers that collect user data.

Microphone Access

Scalita asks for microphone permission for the features that listen to you sing: vocal range detection, the pitch monitor, pitch targeting, the Scale Detector, harmony training, Listen & Repeat, and Humming Bird. In all of them:

  • Audio is processed on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, and we have no servers for it to be transmitted to.
  • Range detection, the pitch monitor, the Scale Detector, Listen & Repeat and Humming Bird do not keep the audio they listen to. It is analysed as it arrives and then discarded.
  • Two features do save audio on your device, because that is the point of them: a practice take in pitch targeting, and the parts of a harmony in harmony training. Those recordings are written to Scalita's own private storage on your phone.
  • Saved recordings stay on your phone until you remove them. Deleting a take, deleting a harmony, or uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Nothing you record is uploaded, shared automatically, or seen by us. Sharing the picture of a finished harmony is something you choose to do, and what leaves the phone is an image.
  • You can skip all of these features and set your range manually.
  • You can revoke microphone permission at any time in your device settings.

In-App Purchases

Scalita offers an optional one-time Pro purchase through Google Play. The purchase transaction is handled entirely by Google Play, and we do not receive or store any payment information. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information about how Google handles purchase data.

Export/Import Feature

You can export your routines and settings as a JSON file to share or backup. This file is created locally on your device. When you share it, you control where it goes. We never see or access these files.

Children's Privacy

Scalita does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we don't collect data, there is no special data handling for children.

Third-Party Services

Scalita uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing - For processing Pro purchases (optional). See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Website and Free Tools

This section covers scalita.com, the website, and the free tools on it: the vocal range test, the sing-in-tune test, the warm-up piano and the voice break finder.

  • Microphone - The vocal range test and the sing-in-tune test use your microphone, and only after you press Start and allow it when your browser asks. The warm-up piano and the voice break finder never use it.
  • Nothing is recorded - Your browser analyzes the sound on your device as it arrives, a few hundredths of a second at a time, and then discards it. No audio is saved, uploaded or sent anywhere, and we have no server that could receive it.
  • Turning it off - When you press Stop or leave the page, the tool releases the microphone.
  • What your browser keeps - A tool that shows your last result, like the vocal range test, keeps it in your browser's local storage on your device, and the site keeps your language choice there too. We can't see either. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes them.
  • Sharing a result - The picture of a result is drawn on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to share or save it, and then it goes where you send it.
  • No analytics or cookies - The website has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it sets no cookies. Its fonts and piano sounds come from scalita.com itself, not from other companies' servers.
  • Links to Google Play - Links to Scalita on Google Play carry a label with the page's language and name, so the Google Play Console can show us, as totals, how many installs came from each page. The label doesn't identify you. Your visit to Google Play is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting - The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor to a GitHub Pages site, for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
