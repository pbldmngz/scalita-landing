The app

What the app adds

The tools run in your browser. The app remembers your range, plays every exercise in your key on a real piano, and with earphones in it draws your pitch as you sing and scores each scale.

  1. 1

    Set your range

    Sing into your phone and Scalita finds your lowest and highest note. Or pick a voice type and skip it.

  2. 2

    Pick a scale or a routine

    21 scales and 15 routines are built in. Not sure which? Answer two questions and Find My Warmup picks for you.

  3. 3

    Sing along

    The piano plays the pattern, moves up a key, and plays it again, all inside the range you set.

21 scales and 15 routines

Major, pentatonic, arpeggios, Rossini patterns, solfège, register bridges, rock and metal, quick warmups, full workouts.

Routines

  • General Warmup Wake up your voice safely, full range ready.
  • Do Re Mi The classic solfège warmup, every note on its name.
  • Gentle / Recovery Light days and post-performance wind-down.
  • Pre-Performance Quick The essentials in under 10 minutes.
  • Range Extension Safely push your upper and lower limits.
  • Power & Volume Cord closure and projection without strain.
  • Agility & Runs Speed and precision for melismatic singing.
  • Breath Control & Stamina Sustained exhalation and phrasing endurance.
  • Bridge Builder Approach, cross, and blend your voice break.
  • Classical / Opera Legato, vowel purity, resonance.
  • Heavy Rock / Distortion Grit and rasp, clean voice first.
  • Vocal Coach I Every standard scale in one place, for teaching.
  • Subharmonic Warmup Find the rattle under your chest voice.
  • Flageolet Bridge Cross into the flute tone above your range.
  • Whistle Explorer A short, quiet visit to the top of the voice.

The last three unlock once you set a range for that register.
