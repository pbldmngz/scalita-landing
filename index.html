Built for real warmups

Not a metronome with a piano skin. Scalita is built around actual vocal exercises that singers use every day. Every exercise shows the syllable to sing and the cues to hold, so you always know what you're working on.

Find My Warmup

Two questions: how your voice feels today, and what you want to work on. Scalita picks the routine and sets the tempo to match. Say you're tired and it takes you somewhere gentle instead of letting you push.

Adaptive Mode

No fixed plan. After every exercise you say how it went - good, meh, hard - and the next one adjusts. Tell it you have ten minutes and it paces the whole session to fit, cooldown included.

New

Subharmonics, Flageolet & Whistle

Most warmup apps stop at chest and head voice. Set a range for each register you actually use and three dedicated routines unlock, built to cross into them: the rattle under your chest voice, the flute tone above your range, and the whistle above that. You can also let flageolet into your normal warmups so it mixes with head voice instead of sitting in its own box.

Scale Detector

Capture a pattern from any source: a YouTube video, a piano, a guitar. Turn it into a scale you can save and reuse. Record, trim, edit note by note, and add it to your library.

Pitch Monitor

See your pitch in real time as you sing. A scrolling trail shows exactly where you land on each note - green when you're on pitch, amber when you drift. Connect earphones and watch yourself improve.

Real Piano Sound

Salamander Grand Piano samples across all 88 keys, A0 to C8. However low or high you sing, there's a real recorded note for it.

Coaching Cues

Every exercise tells you what to hold steady the whole way through, and what changes step by step. Tap any badge to read what it means.

Practice Recorder

Record a take while you practice, then play it back. Hear what you actually sang, not what you remember singing.

Pitch Targeting

Tap any key on the keyboard, hear it, and sing it back. A live trail shows how close you land, one note at a time.

Find Your Range

Sing into your phone to detect your range, or pick a voice type. Mark your voice break too, and exercises build around your register transitions.

Works Offline

No internet needed. Practice backstage, in the car, or at home.

No Ads, No Accounts

Truly free. No tracking, no sign-ups, no interruptions. Just warmups.

Do Re Mi or C D E

Solfege or letter names, whichever you read faster. The whole app speaks English, Spanish, and French.

Build Your Own

Create custom scales and routines from scratch: custom intervals, syllables, tempos, starting notes. Make it yours.
